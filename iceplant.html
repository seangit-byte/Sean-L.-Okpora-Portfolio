IcePlant Management System

A web application that helps an ice plant business manage its customers, inventory, and orders in one place, with a dashboard that shows the key numbers at a glance.

PythonDjangoMySQLPostgreSQL

About the project

IcePlant MS replaces paper records with one system. The owner can sign in, see how the business is doing on the dashboard, keep track of products and stock, save customer details, and follow each order from pending to delivered.

I built the application with Django, designed the database, and connected it to MySQL.

  • Role Developer
  • Framework Django
  • Database MySQL, PostgreSQL
  • Type Web application

What I built

  • Sign in. A login page so only the admin can open the system.
  • Dashboard. Total revenue from delivered orders, number of orders, customers, and products, order status counts, recent orders, and a low stock list.
  • Inventory. Add, edit, search, and delete products with their size, price, and stock.
  • Customers. Save and manage customer names, phone numbers, and addresses.
  • Orders. Create orders and track them as Pending, Confirmed, Delivered, or Cancelled.
