Face Catcher

An interactive game you play with your face and hands. Move your head, open your mouth to catch falling items, and pinch with your fingers to fight the boss.

PythonFace trackingHand trackingWebcam

About the project

Face Catcher uses the webcam to track your face and hands in real time, so there is no keyboard or mouse needed to play. A mesh follows your face, and a circle shows when your mouth is open or closed.

The game runs on a computer with a webcam, so the screenshots below show it in action.

  • Role Developer
  • Language Python
  • Input Webcam
  • Type Interactive game

How to play

  • Move your head to line your mouth up with the falling items.
  • Open your mouth to eat orbs (+10) and stars (+30).
  • Dodge the bombs. Each one costs a life.
  • Beat the boss by pinching bombs with your fingers and dragging them onto it.
