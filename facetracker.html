Face Tracking

AR Face Tracker

A face tracker that reads human emotions from facial expressions. It draws a live mesh over your face and shows your mood on the screen.

PythonFace meshEmotion detectionWebcam

About the project

The AR Face Tracker uses the webcam to find a face, lock onto it, and draw a mesh that follows every movement. It then estimates the mood from the shape of the face, such as neutral, happy, surprised, or sad.

The program runs on a computer with a webcam, so the screenshots below show it in action.

  • Role Developer
  • Language Python
  • Input Webcam
  • Type Computer vision

What it does

  • Face mesh. Draws a live mesh that follows the face.
  • Face lock. Shows how sure the tracker is, as a percentage.
  • Mood detection. Reads the expression and labels it, such as Neutral, Happy, Surprised, or Sad.
  • Color themes. Switch between cyan, magenta, green, and amber while it runs.
